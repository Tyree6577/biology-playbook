# Biology Playbook — Anatomy & Physiology I

An offline collection of eight interactive study activities for introductory college Anatomy & Physiology I.

## Play

Download this repository using **Code → Download ZIP**, extract it, and open **index.html** in a browser. No installation, account, or internet connection is needed to play. Progress lasts while the page stays open; refreshing resets it.

## Activities

1. Cell Arcade — Cell Quest, Spell Lab, and Cell Rescue
2. Systems Rescue
3. Tissue Memory Lab
4. Specimen Safari
5. Tissue Detective — 25 tissues, real micrographs, typed identification, location choices, and missed-slide retries
6. Microscope Mini Golf
7. Biology Review & Mixed Quiz
8. Anatomy Vault — terminology, abdominal regions and organs, visual puzzles, and a timed practice examination

Tissue Detective covers cards 2–26 of the tissue-subtypes study set; the first general Epithelial category is excluded. The games are study aids based on course materials, not a prediction of an instructor’s examination.

## Files

- `index.html` contains the complete collection, including its images and individual games.
- `IMAGE-CREDITS.md` lists the microscopy image sources and licenses. Credits also appear in the game after answers are submitted and in its study guide.
